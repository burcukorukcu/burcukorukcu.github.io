  1. Data Engineer

    MARKT-PILOT

    Mar 2025 – PresentRemote · Berlin, Germany

    I own the silver layer of a Databricks medallion lakehouse for industrial spare-parts data, and the LLM pipeline that matches it.

    • Designed and built an end-to-end parts matching and enrichment pipeline on Databricks: LLM-based manufacturer cleaning, deduplication and verification with web research (SerpAPI), plus a manual-review feedback loop app. 72% of industrial spare parts now match automatically, without manual review.
    • Took the Databricks platform from POC to production: built the silver layer with Lakeflow Spark Declarative Pipelines, combining CDC-based incremental ingestion with materialized views for full refreshes, and turned nested MongoDB documents into analytics-ready tables.
    • Redesigned the gold layer as a star schema with new fact and dimension tables, data contracts and automated quality checks for duplicates, null rates and business rules such as valid price and delivery-time ranges.
    • Built and maintained ETL and orchestration workflows with Dagster on Kubernetes, Airbyte and Apache Spark on AWS (S3, EC2, IAM, ECR), with alerting to Microsoft Teams, then migrated them to Databricks.
    • Implemented Infrastructure-as-Code with Databricks Asset Bundles and Terraform, CI/CD on Azure DevOps, and Unity Catalog access control and lineage for secure data sharing across engineering, analytics and BI.
    • Worked with data scientists, product managers and business stakeholders to turn requirements into reusable data products for internal analytics and executive dashboards.
